# Catalog sources

The catalog contains existing game items, not seller offers. No sale prices, ownership or stock are asserted. Pet names, rarity and origins come from the official Adopt Me and BIG Games indexes. Avatar names and IDs were checked against the Roblox economy API. Original artwork belongs to its respective owners; the source image and credit for every item are recorded below and in public/catalog-items.json.
